My Story
Why supply chain, and why it matters beyond the warehouse

My academic and professional journey has strengthened a simple belief: a well-run supply chain is never just about moving boxes efficiently — it's about food reaching people before it spoils, farmers getting fair value for their harvest, and businesses growing without waste.

I'm particularly drawn to researching resilient logistics systems for agriculture and fisheries, where better supply chain design can directly improve food security, reduce post-harvest losses, and support environmental conservation. It's a niche that sits at the intersection of two things I care about — operational excellence and sustainable development — and it's where I want to spend the next chapter of my career.

Today, that belief shows up in day-to-day work at OnPoint: balancing inventory against demand for brands people trust, and in longer-term projects — from studying reverse logistics for pesticide packaging in the Mekong Delta, to building a knowledge-sharing platform that makes supply chain concepts accessible to Vietnamese students.

Having graduated with a strong academic record, I'm now looking to combine what I've built at OnPoint with further study or research opportunities that let me dig deeper into resilient and sustainable supply chains — whether that's the next role or the next scholarship.
